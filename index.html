About Me

I am Hongming Wang, currently a master's student at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), advised by Prof. Min Yang and Prof. Yinghuan Shi. I will complete my M.S. in Engineering at SUSTech in 2026. I will be joining Nanjing University as a Ph.D. student in Fall 2026. My research focuses on Large Language Models (LLMs), Agents, Deep Research, GUI intelligent interaction, and AI for Science.

My research aims to develop LLM-driven automated systems to facilitate scientific discovery and healthcare applications.

I am always open to research collaborations in LLM and GUI interaction. Feel free to reach out (). Also happy to talk tennis, music, or just life 🎾🎹✈️.
